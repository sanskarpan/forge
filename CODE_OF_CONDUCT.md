# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Forge contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to make participation
welcoming and technically constructive. Treat people with respect, assume good
faith, and focus criticism on code, behavior, and evidence rather than on
individuals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
personal attacks, sexualized conduct, publishing private information, and
deliberate disruption of project spaces.

## Project spaces

This policy applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
commits, documentation, and project-hosted demonstrations. Maintainers may
edit, hide, or close material that violates this policy and may restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do
not include sensitive security details in a conduct report; use the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community
standard, with project-specific reporting guidance.
